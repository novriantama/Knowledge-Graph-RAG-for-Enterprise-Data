Acme Corp (Form 10-K, Item 1 - Business)

Acme Corp is a leading provider of enterprise cloud software, primarily delivering its flagship product, AcmeCloud.
Acme Corp relies heavily on technology frameworks including FastAPI and Pydantic for high-performance API endpoints.
FastAPI and Pydantic depend on underlying open-source data validation libraries maintained by Supplier X.

Acme Corp operates a major European subsidiary named Acme EU GmbH located in Berlin, Germany.
Acme EU GmbH complies with European Union Cyber Resilience Regulations (EU CRA) through cloud infrastructure certified under ISO 27001 provided by AWS Germany.
