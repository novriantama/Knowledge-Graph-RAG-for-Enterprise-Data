Security Frameworks & Regulatory Compliance - Acme Global Systems

1. Regulatory Compliance Standards
- Acme EU GmbH COMPLIES_WITH European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for data privacy and user consent management.
- Acme EU GmbH COMPLIES_WITH European Union Cyber Resilience Act (EU CRA) through automated software bill of materials (SBOM) scanning and CVE vulnerability reporting.
- Acme Global Systems COMPLIES_WITH SOC 2 Type II compliance framework across all US and EU operations.
- Payment & Billing Service (Service-104) COMPLIES_WITH PCI-DSS v4.0 standard for payment card security.

2. Audit Compliance Dependencies
- GDPR compliance COMPLIES_WITH / REQUIRES PostgreSQL Database data encryption at rest using AWS KMS.
- EU CRA compliance DEPENDS_ON vulnerability tracking of open-source libraries maintained by Supplier-X (AnyIO & pydantic-core).
- SOC 2 Type II audit verification DEPENDS_ON automated monitoring from Datadog Agent and Cloudflare audit logs.

3. Incident Response & Supply Chain Governance
- In the event of a critical security advisory affecting packages maintained by Supplier-X, User Auth Service (Service-102) and Payment & Billing Service (Service-104) must undergo immediate hotfix patching within 24 hours.
- Failure of Supplier-X packages directly impacts EU CRA regulatory compliance for Acme EU GmbH.
